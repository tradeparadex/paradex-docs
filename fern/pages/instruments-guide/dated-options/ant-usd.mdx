## Index and settlement price

ANT options are priced off the **Paradex ANT Spot Oracle Price**, the same index used by the [ANT-USD-PERP](/trading/instruments-guide/futures/trad-fi/nvda-usd-perp) perpetual, and settle at the **30-minute TWAP** of that price ending at expiry. The Spot Oracle Price is computed as a weighted median of the sources below. Each source has a score; its weight is that score divided by the total score of the sources available at the time, so weights differ between market open and closing hours.

| Source | Description | Score | Availability |
|--------|-------------|:-----:|:------------:|
| Pyth | [Pyth ANT equity index](https://app.pyth.com/explore/Equity.US.ANT%2FUSD) | 3 | Open hours |
| Stork | [Stork ANT oracle price](https://data.stork.network/assets/ANTUSD) | 2 | Open hours |
| Binance | [Binance ANT index price](https://www.binance.com/en/futures/ANTUSDT) | 1 | Open hours |
| Bybit | [Bybit ANT index price](https://www.bybit.com/en/trade/usdt/ANTUSDT) | 1 | 24/7 |
| Hyperliquid | [Hyperliquid ANT index price](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade/xyz:ANT) | 1 | 24/7 |
| Lighter | [Lighter ANT index price](https://app.lighter.xyz/trade/ANT) | 0.7 | 24/7 |
| Closing Price | Paradex ANT spot price at the market closing timestamp | 0.5 | Closing hours |
| Internal Price | EMA of the Paradex ANT-USD-PERP mark price with a 1-hour half-life, initialised at the Closing Price | 0.5 | Closing hours |

### Market hours

ANT options follow the equities session: open 24/5 from Sunday 8:00 PM ET to Friday 8:00 PM ET, including pre-market, regular, post-market and overnight sessions. Weekends are closing hours.

### Example

To find the Spot Oracle Price, sort the source prices from lowest to highest and add up their scores in that order. The Spot Oracle Price is the first price at which the running total goes above half of the total score. If the running total lands exactly on half, the price is the average of that source's price and the next one. A source with no fresh price is left out, and the total is recomputed from the sources that remain.

The prices below are illustrative.

### Mark and settlement price

- **Mark price**: the Spot Oracle Price is the spot input $S$ to the synthetic forward used by the [Black-76 mark price](/trading/dated-options/mark-price).
- **Settlement price**: options settle at 16:00 UTC on the expiry date using a [30-minute TWAP](/trading/dated-options#settlement) of the Spot Oracle Price ending at expiry.

See [TradFi Price Oracle](/trading/tradfi-markets) for the methodology across all TradFi markets.
