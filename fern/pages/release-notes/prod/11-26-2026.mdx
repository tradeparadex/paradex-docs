## v1.173.7

<ChangelogTags tags={["UI"]} />

#### UI

* `Withdraw` and `Deposit` forms no longer show or use a fee quoted for an earlier amount or route.
* Fixed a blank page showing instead of the crash message when the app hits an unexpected error.
* Order notifications for option markets the page has not loaded no longer go missing.
* Trading sessions now open with a fast device clock, and a refused session no longer blocks wallet login.
* `Fordefi` wallets now log in with an EVM account only.
* Market names drop the `USD` suffix and show a `perp`, `spot` or `option` tag instead.
* The `Vaults` and `Portfolio` pages download much less data while open.
* The `Portfolio`, `Vaults` and `Markets` pages load faster and use less memory.
* `Hyperlane` deposits now stop before any wallet prompt when the wallet cannot pay the bridge fee.
* Switch options instruments straight from the order panel header with a searchable dropdown.
