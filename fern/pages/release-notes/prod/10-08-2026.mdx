<ChangelogTags tags={["API", "UI"]} />

#### API

* Equity and commodity options list the next two dailies, settling at the exchange's own option close.

#### UI

* Vault close, settings and withdraw now work for vaults run from an EVM wallet.
* Shared payoff posts now tag structures `Bullish`, `Bearish`, `Long Vol` or `Short Vol` from their full payoff.
