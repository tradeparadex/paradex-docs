## v1.173.2

<ChangelogTags tags={["UI", "API"]} />

#### UI

* `Transfer` names your sub accounts and vault accounts the same way the account menu does.
* The `Deposit` form now opens without reloading and enables `Deposit` sooner.
* `Hyperlane` deposits now stop before any wallet prompt when the source chain balance is too low.
* Accounts with empty isolated margin sub accounts can now trade on a session and add `passkeys`.
* The options order builder stays on `Market` or `Limit` when you click a different strike.
* Fixed trading sessions failing to open when the device clock runs slightly fast.
* `Withdraw` and `Transfer` show your available amount sooner and never show 0 while it loads.
* Fixed the order size changing by one unit after switching between buy and sell.
* `Deposit` now shows your wallet balances faster, and at once when reopened.
* The trade page downloads less JavaScript before it can render.

#### API

* Revoking a `builder approval` now cancels that builder's open orders right away.
* `Deposit` and `Withdraw` fee quotes load faster when reopening the funding modals or editing the amount.
* Requests that cannot sign for an account no longer use up that account's `RPC` rate limit.
