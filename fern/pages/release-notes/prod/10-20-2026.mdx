## v1.168.2

<ChangelogTags tags={["UI"]} />

#### UI

* You can now send funds to a new Paradex account that has not been funded yet.
* `RFQ quotes` show Insufficient liquidity and `Best Price` when `Deribit` has no price for the structure.
* `P&L share cards` are now `square`, so several of them read cleanly in one post on X.
* `Closed Perp P&L` share cards now show your `leverage` and your return on `margin`.
* Saving addresses happens in its own view, and Transfer to now works as a dropdown.
* The options `RFQ` leg table drops its redundant `Product` column and shows `short strategy names`.
