| Ticker | \$DIME |
| Total supply | 1,000,000,000 \$DIME |
| Circulating supply | See [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/paradex) for live data |

| Networks | Ethereum, Paradex Chain, Solana |

\$DIME is natively issued on Ethereum and bridged to Paradex Chain and Solana. On Paradex Chain it is used for staking, fee payments, and trading on the [DIME-USD spot market](/trading/instruments-guide/spot/dime-usd).

## Buybacks

Net revenue generated from protocol fees will be used to buy back $DIME and/or fund ecosystem growth initiatives. Buybacks are executed periodically (e.g. Daily TWAP) using available net revenue.

The account that conducts buybacks is `0x3f38009a58bfc930a399ef9670ee60b355d9d22ea79709f2243182989d16709` and its $DIME balance can be viewed [here](https://app.paradex.trade/dime-fund)

## Fee Discounts

\$DIME fee discounts apply to **Pro taker fees on Perps and Spot**. See [Trading Fees](/trading/trading-fees) for the full schedule.

### Pay Fees in \$DIME

Pay fees in \$DIME for a flat 40% discount on your Pro taker fees. Fees are deducted from your spot \$DIME balance at the \$DIME ↔ USDC mark price at transaction time.

<Tip title="Example" icon="fa-solid fa-lightbulb">
A Pro user who stakes 100K \$DIME (15% staking discount) and pays fees in \$DIME (40% \$DIME fee discount) gets a total discount of 55% (15% + 40%), applied to their volume based Pro taker fee.
</Tip>

## Gas

$DIME is intended to be the gas token for Paradex Chain (DimeVM), a general-purpose smart contract platform powered by Cairo. DimeVM shares state with the Paradex Exchange, enabling a composable, high-throughput execution environment where onchain applications can interact with the liquidity and privacy infrastructure built on Paradex.
