## v1.172.5

<ChangelogTags tags={["UI", "API"]} />

#### UI

* `Deposit` appears right after `sign-in` instead of waiting for sub-accounts to load.
* `Portfolio margin` accounts now see margin requirements and liquidation prices closer to what the exchange applies.
* Available to Trade no longer overstates buying power for accounts holding options.
* The futures chart opens at its saved zoom instead of redrawing from a squeezed view.


#### API

* A WebSocket `order.modify` whose size is at or below the filled size returns an invalid params error with the reason, not an internal error.
* `RFQ margin` estimates for assets other than BTC and ETH no longer overstate the margin required.
* `Vault` withdrawals no longer stay pending after the funds have reached your account.
