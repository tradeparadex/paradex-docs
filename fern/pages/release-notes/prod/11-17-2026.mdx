## v1.172.1

<ChangelogTags tags={["UI"]} />

#### UI

* Fixed Deposit, Withdraw and Transfer buttons sometimes disappearing until the page was reloaded.
* The options page and RFQ builder respond faster on slower computers.
* Futures and options pages load faster by reading only the option markets they show.
* The options chart now switches underlyings faster from the RFQ builder.
* Fixed transfers and withdrawals from EVM wallet accounts sometimes failing with an out-of-gas error.
* Fixed a strategy's RFQ button opening a single leg instead of the whole structure.
* Fixed the app repeatedly requesting a missing VTF in the background.
* RFQ size slider responds across the whole track on small accounts and keeps the thumb on the chosen size.
* Email and social logins no longer fail to link the wallet or get signed out on reload.
