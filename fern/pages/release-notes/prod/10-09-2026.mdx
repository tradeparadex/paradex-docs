## v1.166.8

<ChangelogTags tags={["API", "UI"]} />

#### API

* Layerswap, airdrop and vault donation transfers that were missing now appear in transfer history.
* DIME staked from or un-staked into a Paradex account now appears in transfer history.
* Revoked subkeys can no longer place or cancel orders over an open websocket.
* Apps can request a USDC deposit address on Arbitrum, Base or Ethereum for the user's own Paradex account.
* RFQ trading can now only be enabled from your main account.

#### UI

* Closing positions with RFQ now loads the exact position sizes, in contracts.
* RFQs now play a sound on execute confirm and on fill.
* Owner-only account actions now ask EVM users for a wallet signature when needed.
